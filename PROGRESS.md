- **Task 13:** `sliceTesseract` slices each face's 4 edges directly (per PLAN.md), not a general polygon-hyperplane intersection — a face crossed by `w=w0` always has exactly 2 of its 4 edges cross (convexity), so `edgeCrossing` returning 0 or 1 point per edge and collecting exactly-2-per-face into one output segment is sufficient; no convex-hull step needed. `aw === bw` (an edge that doesn't move in `w`) short-circuits to "no crossing" rather than computing `0/0` — those edges belong to faces where `w` is a *fixed* axis, and any face actually intersected by the hyperplane has its 2 crossings supplied by its other, `w`-varying edges instead, so this never drops a real crossing.
